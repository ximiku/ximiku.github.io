<div class="about-intro">
  <p>
    I am an undergraduate student majoring in <strong>Bioinformatics</strong> at the
    <a href="https://www.bio.pku.edu.cn/en/index/index.html" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">School of Life Sciences</a>,
    <a href="https://english.pku.edu.cn" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Peking University</a>,
    Beijing, P.R. China. My interests lie at the interface of
    <strong>biotechnology and AI for Science</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    I currently pursue research on <strong>large language models</strong> with
    <a href="https://yangtonghome.github.io" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Prof. Tong Yang</a> in
    <a href="https://huggingface.co/PKU-DS-LAB" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">PKU-DS-LAB</a>.
    I also work on <strong>B-cell immunity</strong> at
    <a href="https://biopic.pku.edu.cn" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">BIOPIC</a> in
    <a href="https://yunlongcaolab.com" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Prof. Yunlong Cao</a>'s lab.
    Previously, I contributed to <strong>cancer network genomics</strong> projects in Fenglin Liu's lab.
  </p>

  <p>
    My broader goal is to build <strong>computational and experimental tools</strong> for measuring, modeling, and
    engineering biological systems. This includes reading biological variation from omics and cohort-level data; modeling biological function with language models and other AI-driven
    approaches; and engineering biological processes through genome editing, molecular delivery, and system-level
    optimization. I also maintain
    <a href="https://ximiku.github.io/HanaBio-Archive/" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">HanaBio Archive</a>,
    a biology competition archive site.
  </p>
</div>
