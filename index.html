I am an undergraduate student majoring in Bioinformatics at the School of Life Sciences, Peking University, Beijing, P.R. China. My interests lie at the interface of biotechnology and AI for Science.

I currently pursue research on large language models with Prof. Tong Yang in PKU-DS-LAB. I also work on B-cell immunity at BIOPIC in Prof. Yunlong Cao's lab. Previously, I contributed to cancer network genomics projects in Fenglin Liu's lab.

My broader goal is to build computational and experimental tools for measuring, modeling, and engineering biological systems. This includes reading biological variation from omics and cohort-level data; modeling biological function with language models and other AI-driven approaches; and engineering biological processes through genome editing, molecular delivery, and system-level optimization. I also maintain HanaBio Archive, a biology competition archive site.
